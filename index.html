Our Himalayan Heritage

High-Altitude Botanical Alchemy

Rooted in the pristine valleys of the Himalayas, Lumière Beauty bridges centuries of traditional South Asian botanical knowledge with contemporary peptide cosmetic science.

The Atelier Story

Born in Kathmandu, Elevated for the World

Founded in 2024 at our Durbar Marg salon atelier, Lumière Beauty was conceived from a single observation: the unique Himalayan climate—dry mountain winds, intense ultraviolet exposure, and drastic temperature shifts—requires skincare that heals, fortifies, and illuminates without heaviness.

We collaborate directly with indigenous botanical farming cooperatives in Manang, Mustang, and Dolpa who sustainably hand-harvest wild Damask roses, seabuckthorn berries, and alpine edelweiss at altitudes exceeding 3,000 meters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers from Our Chemists

Everything you need to know regarding our Himalayan botanicals, delivery timeline across Nepal, and order policies.

What is the standard delivery timeline across Nepal? expand_more
We provide express 24-hour courier delivery inside Kathmandu Valley (Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur). For major cities such as Pokhara, Biratnagar, Chitwan, Dharan, Butwal, and Nepalgunj, shipments arrive safely within 2 to 3 business days. Remote districts arrive within 4 to 5 business days via tracked Nepal Post insured service.
Are Lumière Beauty formulations suitable for sensitive South Asian skin? expand_more
Yes, rigorously so. Every formulation is dermatologist tested, non-comedogenic, and hypoallergenic. We refrain from using harsh synthetic perfumes, parabens, sulfates, and drying alcohols. Our alpine edelweiss and high-altitude sea buckthorn extracts are specifically chosen to soothe reactive skin and barrier damage caused by dry Kathmandu winters and intense monsoon humidity.
How do I qualify for Free Shipping across Nepal? expand_more
All orders exceeding NPR 4,000 automatically qualify for complimentary insured courier delivery across all 7 provinces of Nepal. For orders below NPR 4,000, a flat fee of NPR 200 is charged to cover secure protective packaging and parcel tracking.
How does the 14-day shade exchange policy work? expand_more
If your chosen cushion foundation or concealer shade does not complement your natural undertones, simply notify our Kathmandu concierge within 14 days of delivery. We will arrange a door-to-door courier swap for your corrected shade.
Are all products 100% cruelty-free and vegan? expand_more
Absolutely. Lumière Beauty is proud to be certified Leaping Bunny cruelty-free. None of our ingredients or finished cosmetics are tested on animals. Furthermore, our brushes utilize ultra-soft DermaFlex synthetic micro-bristles rather than animal hair.
What payment methods are supported in the demo experience? expand_more
This academic web development project simulates popular Nepali digital wallets (eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS) as well as Cash on Delivery (COD) and Credit/Debit Cards. Please note that all transactions are simulated for demonstration purposes and no real charges will occur.

University Project Documentation

LUMIÈRE BEAUTY — Web Development Project

Module Requirements & GitHub Pages Compatibility Guide

1. Module Compliance Summary

• Strict Vanilla Architecture: 100% pure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. Zero React, zero TypeScript dependencies, zero Tailwind runtime or external heavy frameworks.

• Separation of Concerns: index.html (Markup), css/style.css (Styles), js/products.js (Data), and js/app.js (Behavior).

• Local & GitHub Pages Compatibility: All resource paths use relative resolution (./css/style.css, ./js/app.js, etc.). The website runs directly when double-clicking index.html in a browser without any dev server needed!

2. Complete Feature Inventory

• 28 Full Products: High-altitude Himalayan skincare, lipsticks, mineral cushions, baked highlighters, fragrance, and tools with NPR pricing.

• Dynamic Shopping Bag & Free Shipping Calculator: Real-time recalculation of subtotals, free shipping threshold (NPR 4,000), coupon code engine (GLOW15 = 15% off, FIRSTGLOW = NPR 500 off), and quantity steppers.

• Wishlist & LocalStorage: Full persistence across browser refreshes.

• Complete Checkout & Invoice Generator: Nepal province & city validation, simulated payment methods (eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, COD), and auto-save into Patron Order History.

3. Moodle Submission Instructions

1. Compress this project folder into a .zip file containing index.html at the root, along with the css/ and js/ folders.

2. Upload the ZIP archive to your university Moodle submission portal.

3. Your assessor can unzip and double-click index.html to test every feature instantly.
